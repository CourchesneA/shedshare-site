Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy explains what personal information the ShedShare Android app collects, why, who can see it, where it is stored and how to use your rights. The French version is the reference version.

1. Who we are

ShedShare is a free app published by Anthony Courchesne, in Québec (Canada). Anthony Courchesne is also the person in charge of the protection of personal information.

For any question or request: titocourch@gmail.com.

3. Consent

When you sign in with Google, you consent to the use of your information as this policy describes. You can withdraw your consent at any time: leave your groups or ask us to delete your account (section 7).

4. Who sees your information

  • Members of your groups see your display name, your tools and their photos, the state of borrows and the history of the tools of the group.
  • People outside your groups see nothing of your groups. Access rules in the database enforce this.
  • Anyone who has the invite code of a group can join it. Share the code only with people you trust.

5. Service providers and where data is stored

We use these providers. They process your information only to provide their service:

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and photos. Its servers are in the United States (Amazon Web Services, us-east-1 region). Your information is therefore stored outside Québec and can be subject to the laws of the United States.
  • Google confirms your identity when you sign in. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

6. How long we keep information

  • We keep your information as long as your account exists.
  • When you leave a group, the app deletes your tools in that group, their photos and their history, and all your requests in that group.
  • When you stop sharing a tool, it is no longer visible, but it stays in the database with its history. To delete it, leave the group or write to us.
  • When you sign out, the app removes your session from the device. Your data stays in your account.
  • The app excludes its data from Android backups and from transfers to another device.

7. Your rights and deleting your account

You can ask for access to your information, its correction or its deletion, and withdraw your consent. Write to titocourch@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account.

To delete your account, write "Delete my ShedShare account". We delete your account, profile, memberships, tools, photos and requests within 30 days. We answer every request within 30 days.

If our answer does not satisfy you, you can file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

8. Security

Traffic between the app and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Photos are in a private storage space that only group members can read. No method is perfectly secure. If a privacy incident presents a risk of serious harm, we notify the people concerned and the Commission d'accès à l'information.

9. Minimum age

ShedShare is for people aged 14 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about children under 14. If this happens, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We can change this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. If a change is important, we tell members before it takes effect.

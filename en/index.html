ShedShare

See which tools your friends have, and borrow them in a few taps.

Groups of friends

Create a group and share its invite code. Only members see what is in the group.

Your tools at a glance

Add your tools with a description and a photo. Everyone sees what is available or lent out.

Ask, approve, return

Ask for a tool, the owner approves or declines, then one of you marks it returned.

Always up to date

Changes show right away for the other members, with the history of each tool.

Get the app

ShedShare is a free Android app with no ads. It is in French and English.

  • You need a phone with Android 8.0 or newer and a Google account.
  • The app is not on Google Play. The person who invites you sends you the install file and the code of your group.
ShedShare uses your Google account only to sign you in and show your name to your friends. Read the privacy policy and the terms of service.
